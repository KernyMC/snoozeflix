Money, reimagined

Fair by design, not gambling.

✔ The pool is never paid out as cash and never refunded to the flaker.

✔ A locked stake covers your next 3 days, so nobody can flake and run.

✔ Withdrawals have a cooling period and can be cancelled.

✔ Penalties double per flake but are capped, and a balance never goes negative.

✔ A full pool nobody spends in 7 days goes to charity.
